Kalinga Architecture

Kalinga architecture is a distinct regional style of Hindu temple architecture that flourished in ancient Kalinga (modern-day Odisha, India) from the 6th to 16th centuries CE. As a sub-style of the Nagara school, it is characterized by tall, curvilinear towers (shikharas), intricate stone carvings, and a tripartite structural layout: Rekha Deula (sanctum), Pidha Deula (assembly hall), and sometimes Khakhara Deula.

KIIT BBSR

Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology (KIIT) is a premier, private deemed-to-be university located in the Patia neighborhood of Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India. Established in 1992 as a modest vocational training center, it has grown into a massive educational township spanning 25 square kilometers with 23 interconnected eco-friendly campuses.
