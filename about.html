About Odisha

Odisha, formerly Orissa, is a vibrant state on India’s eastern coast known for its rich cultural heritage, ancient temples, and natural beauty. Capitalized at Bhubaneswar, it spans 155,707 km² and serves as a major industrial hub with significant mineral resources, a 485 km coastline, and a population over 41 million.

Jgannath Temple

Jagannath Puri is a sacred coastal city in Odisha, India, famous for its 12th-century Shree Jagannath Temple. Dedicated to Lord Jagannath (a form of Lord Vishnu), his brother Balabhadra, and sister Subhadra, it is one of the four highly revered Char Dham pilgrimage sites. The city sits on the Bay of Bengal, roughly 60 kilometers south of the state capital, Bhubaneswar.

The Shadowless Dome: The main dome of the temple casts no shadow on the ground at any time of day.The Reverse Flag: The flag atop the spire consistently flutters in the exact opposite direction of the wind.The Silent Entrance: Upon stepping inside the main entrance (Singhadwara), the sound of the ocean waves completely disappears.The Kitchen Wonder: The temple houses the world's largest kitchen. Food is cooked in seven earthen pots stacked directly on top of each other; the topmost pot always cooks first.The Divine Heart: Legends state that the hidden Brahma Padartha (sacred core substance) inside the idol is the actual, still-beating heart of Lord Krishna

Lingaraj Temple

Lingaraj Temple is the largest, oldest, and most prominent architectural landmark in Bhubaneswar, the capital city of Odisha. Constructed in the 11th century CE by King Jajati Keshari of the Somavamsi dynasty, this active place of worship is dedicated to Lord Shiva. It stands as the finest surviving example of the medieval Kalinga architectural style.

Unlike most orthodox Shiva temples, the deity here is worshipped as Harihara—a unique combined manifestation of both Lord Shiva (Hari) and Lord Vishnu (Hara). This synthesis reflects the historical harmony between Shaivism and Vaishnavism sects in Odisha. The central Swayambhu (self-manifested) stone phallus is a massive unshaped granite block measuring 8 feet in diameter.

Nandankanan Zoological Park

Nandankanan Zoological Park is a premier 1,080-acre zoo and botanical garden located in the Chandaka forest region, roughly 15 kilometers north of Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Literally meaning "The Garden of Heaven," it was established in 1960 and became the first Indian zoo to join the World Association of Zoos and Aquariums (WAZA). It is globally renowned for its pioneering white tiger breeding programs and its highly successful captive breeding of endangered gharials

Bhubaneswar Smart City

Bhubaneswar made history by ranking first nationally in India’s Smart Cities Mission challenge. Managed by Bhubaneswar Smart City Limited (BSCL), the initiative leverages tech-driven, citizen-centric infrastructure across its urban network.

Bhubaneswar Metro Rail: A massive elevated corridor with 20 stations is under active development, bridging major transit zones from Biju Patnaik International Airport to Trisulia

SUIIT

Puri Beach, also known as Golden Beach, is a world-famous stretch of shoreline located along the Bay of Bengal in the spiritual city of Puri, Odisha. Situated just 2 km away from the iconic Shree Jagannath Temple, it perfectly blends coastal leisure with profound religious significance, as Hindu pilgrims traditionally take a holy dip in its waters.

The beach is highly celebrated for its unique Blue Flag certification—an international eco-label honoring its pristine environmental, safety, and cleanliness standards. It features remarkable sand art displays created by world-renowned local artist Sudarshan Pattnaik.

GM University

Biju Patnaik International Airport (BBI), also known as Bhubaneswar Airport, is the primary aviation gateway to the state of Odisha. Situated in the capital city of Bhubaneswar, it is located roughly 60 km away from Puri Beach, serving as the most convenient transit point for travelers arriving by air to visit the Jagannath Temple and the coastline.

KALINGA HOSPITAL

Kalinga Hospital Limited (KHL) is a premier 250-bed multi-super specialty tertiary care hospital located in Chandrasekharpur, Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Established in 1990 by a philanthropic group of Non-Resident Odia (NRO) doctors from the USA, it holds the distinction of being Odisha’s first corporate hospital.

The hospital offers a comprehensive range of medical services:Cardiology & Cardiac Surgery: Outfitted with an advanced catheterization laboratory, executing adult and pediatric life-saving procedures like TAVI.Renal Sciences: Highly recognized for Nephrology, Urology, and has completed over 500 successful kidney transplants.Orthopedics & Spine Surgery: Advanced care including robotic joint replacements and sports science.Neurosciences: Features specialized facilities for keyhole neurosurgery, advanced spine treatments, and neurointerventions.

Odisha State Museum

Odisha State Museum is a museum in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. In its original form it was established in 1932 and later moved to the current building in 1960.

The location is perfect for photography, sightseeing, and enjoying peaceful natural beauty.

Ratha yatra

Ratha Yatra (Chariot Festival) is a major Hindu festival centered around the public procession of massive deities on ornately decorated wooden chariots. While celebrated across various regions, its most historic and largest manifestation occurs in the coastal temple city of Puri, Odisha, India

The Deities: The procession honors Lord Jagannath (a form of Krishna/Vishnu), his older brother Balabhadra, and their younger sister Subhadra.The Journey: The three sibling deities travel on separate temple-sized chariots from their primary residence, the Jagannath Temple, to their garden palace at the Gundicha Temple, located approximately 3 kilometers away.
